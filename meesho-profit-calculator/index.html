Why Meesho looks profitable until RTO hits

Low headline fees can hide the real cost: failed deliveries, returns, and reverse logistics. A unit that “sold” but came back still burned packaging, shipping, and opportunity cost.

What to model for Meesho

  • Platform / commission fees for your category and programme.
  • Forward shipping and packaging on every attempt, not only delivered orders.
  • RTO and return rates by category—often much higher than Amazon/Flipkart fashion.
  • Boost / ad spend when you buy visibility.
  • COGS that still leave your warehouse on RTO cycles.

Multi-channel reality

If you also sell on Amazon or Flipkart, compare channels with the same COGS and return definitions. Otherwise Meesho volume can look attractive while destroying cash.
