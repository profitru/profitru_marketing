Why Flipkart disbursements mislead

Portal settlements show what Flipkart paid after marketplace deductions. True SKU profit still needs landed cost, allocated ads, cancellations, return damage, and GST treatment—often missing from the same export.

Line items to include

  • Commission and fixed fees by category and price band.
  • Shipping and reverse logistics: forward, RVP, and unsellable returns.
  • Cancellations and failed deliveries that still consume pick/pack cost.
  • Advertising allocated to listings that consumed spend.
  • GST alignment between invoices, credit notes, and your returns.

When Flipkart is one of several channels

Many brands run Flipkart beside Amazon, Meesho, and their own store. Margin discipline means the same COGS and return definitions everywhere—otherwise you over-invest in whichever channel looks friendliest on paper.
