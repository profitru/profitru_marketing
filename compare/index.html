When to choose which

Recommended for most multi-channel SMBs

Choose Profitru if…

You need more than analytics or OMS alone: real profit per SKU across channels, ads in the same view, returns/QC, video pack proofs, offline sales, and AI — without API wait.

Choose Unicommerce if…

Your #1 pain is heavy fulfilment: live inventory sync, multi-warehouse routing, and marketplace order ops at high volume.

Choose Sellerboard if…

You are Amazon-only and want deep Amazon PPC / reimbursement analytics more than India multi-channel profit + ops.

Need a heavy OMS and margin truth? Many teams keep Unicommerce for warehouse sync and add Profitru for real profit — or start with Profitru alone if Excel is the current “system.”
