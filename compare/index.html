When to choose which

Choose Unicommerce if…

Your pain is fulfilment at scale: live inventory sync, multi-warehouse routing, and marketplace order ops across many channels.

Choose Sellerboard if…

You are Amazon-first and need deep Amazon profit, PPC, and reimbursement-style analytics more than India multi-channel ops.

Choose Profitru if…

You need real profit per SKU across Amazon, Flipkart & more — fees, ads, returns, GST — plus inventory/ops, video pack proofs, and offline sales, without waiting on marketplace APIs.

Many sellers use more than one tool. Profitru can sit beside a heavy OMS when your gap is margin truth — not another warehouse UI.
